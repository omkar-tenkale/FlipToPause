Flip to Pause

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

Flip to Pause does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal user data.

  • No Internet Access: The app (`dev.omkartenkale.fliptopause`) does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. It cannot send any information over the internet.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporters, or advertising frameworks included in the app.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide any personal details to use the app.

2. How Sensor Data is Used

To provide its core functionality, Flip to Pause reads orientation data from your device's built-in gravity or accelerometer sensor:

  • Sensor readings are processed entirely in real-time in your device's memory (RAM) to determine whether to pause or resume active media playback.
  • Sensor data is never recorded, logged, saved to disk, or shared with any external party.

3. Local Storage (Device Preferences)

Your app settings (Pause Delay, Tilt Sensitivity, and Flip Direction) are stored locally on your device using standard Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is automatically deleted if you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage.

4. Permissions Used

Flip to Pause requests only the minimum system permissions required to function:

  • Foreground Service: Allows the app to continue detecting device orientation while the screen is off or while you are using other media apps.
  • Post Notifications: Displays a persistent status notification while the service is active so you can see its status and turn it off at any time.
  • Vibrate: Provides subtle haptic feedback when toggling settings or when playback pauses/resumes.
  • Ignore Battery Optimizations: Optional setting that helps prevent the system from unexpectedly stopping the background service.

5. Children's Privacy & Open Source

Because Flip to Pause does not collect any data from any user, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global privacy regulations. Flip to Pause is open source, allowing anyone to inspect the source code and verify that no data collection occurs.
